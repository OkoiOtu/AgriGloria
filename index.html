About AgriGloria Farms

Our journey in sustainable farming and animal husbandry

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, healthy livestock through sustainable farming practices that contribute to food security and economic growth in Cross River State and beyond.

Our Vision

To become the leading agricultural enterprise in Southern Nigeria, setting standards in animal welfare, sustainable farming, and customer satisfaction.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, AgriGloria Farms began as a small family-owned venture in Ayaebam Village, Akamkpa. What started with a few chickens and goats has grown into a reputable livestock farm serving individuals, businesses, and communities across Cross River State.

Our name "AgriGloria" combines Agriculture and Glory, reflecting our commitment to bringing glory to farming through excellence, innovation, and sustainable practices. We believe in farming that honors both the land and the people it serves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering, delivery, and our livestock

Orders & Delivery

How do I place an order for livestock?

You can place an order by calling our sales lines at +234 803 123 4567, sending a WhatsApp message, or using the contact form on this website. Our team will guide you through availability, pricing, and delivery options.

What areas do you deliver to, and what are the costs?

We provide reliable delivery primarily across Cross River State. Delivery to neighboring states can be arranged. Costs vary based on distance and order size; we will provide a clear quote before you confirm your order.

Can I visit the farm to see the animals before purchasing?

Absolutely! We encourage farm visits by prior appointment (see our contact section for hours). It's the best way to see our sustainable practices and healthy livestock firsthand.

Livestock & Quality

Are your animals vaccinated and disease-free?

Yes. Animal welfare is our priority. All our livestock receive proper veterinary care, including necessary vaccinations and regular health checks, to ensure they are healthy and robust.

Do you offer advice on housing and care for the animals after purchase?

Yes, we do! We provide basic after-sales guidance on care, feeding, and housing. We want you and your new animals to thrive.

What breeds of goats/pigs/chickens do you raise?

We raise quality, productive breeds suited to our region. For specific breed information and recommendations for your needs, please contact our team directly.

Pricing & Payment

Do you offer discounts for bulk or wholesale orders?

Yes, we have special pricing for bulk purchases and established partnerships with businesses and cooperatives. Contact our sales team for a customized wholesale quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers and cash. Payment details are confirmed at the time of order. For large orders, we require a 50% deposit with the balance due before delivery.

What is your return or replacement policy?

We stand by the quality of our livestock. If there are any health issues upon delivery, please contact us immediately. We'll arrange a replacement or refund according to our quality assurance policy.

Didn't find your answer? Our team is ready to help with any specific questions about our livestock or services.
